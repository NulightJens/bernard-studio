# Bernard's rules

These hold for every piece the studio makes. Claude checks them before showing him anything, and `studio qa`
measures the ones a machine can measure.

1. **His words only.** Every line in a piece is something he really said, in his own voice. On-screen text is his
   words (the exact phrase, or a shorter piece of it) plus a style's few fixed labels. Numbers he speaks may show as
   digits. Nobody writes new lines for him, and his voice is never cloned or changed.
2. **Voice first.** His voice sits on top. Music sits about 18 dB under him and effects 24 to 30 dB under, and both
   dip while he talks. The finished file is -14 LUFS with true peak at or under -1.5 dBTP.
3. **Cut only on real pauses.** A line starts and ends in a measured silence, never mid-word, and every line is a
   complete thought.
4. **One red.** Blood Red `#B91C1C` (`#C42727` over footage) marks one word or one mark per frame, never two.
5. **His kit.** Obsidian blacks, cream paper `#F1EBDF`, warm white `#EEE9DF`, iron gray `#2E2E2E`. Fonts: Cormorant
   Garamond (italic for emphasis), Raleway 800 capitals for captions, Special Elite for labels, Permanent Marker two or
   three times a video and never twice in one frame. Brush strokes from `brushes/`.
6. **No made-up pictures.** Pictures are his real footage, his real screens, his kit photos, free-to-use (CC0) object
   renders, or shapes drawn by code. No AI-generated images or video, and he is never painted, drawn or cloned.
7. **Safe zones.** On reels and animated pieces, text stays 80 px from the sides, below the top 220 px (the phone's
   status bar) and above the bottom 300 px (Instagram's buttons and caption). On carousels, 80 px from the sides.
8. **Nothing goes out on its own.** The studio never posts or uploads. Finished files land in his `Finished` folder
   with a caption draft made from his words, and he posts them himself.

Bernard Ablola · @bernardablola · style board

Your style

Samurai discipline, Zen restraint. Dark, moody and cinematic: deep obsidian blacks, cool ink-blue shadow, one blood-red note per frame, and an elegant serif that carries the weight of an old text. Bernard is an author, strategist and creative: Eastern philosophy, Western strategy, skate-culture grit. His videos feel like a short film from an essayist, not an influencer. They must never look glossy, bouncy, cluttered or like stock "unlock your potential" content.

Typography

Cinematic, not theatrical. Intellectual, not pretentious. Creative, not chaotic.

Four faces, one job each
Display · the voice
Aa
Cormorant Garamond
Cinematic headlines. Elegant, editorial, sophisticated. Italic 700; upright 600 for titles and quotes.
  • Hooks, thesis lines, words behind the head
  • The one red word inside a caption
  • Card titles and book quotes
  • Paragraphs, or anything under 48px on video
Body · the workhorse
Aa
Raleway
Captions, labels and supporting text. Clean, modern, minimal. 800 caps for captions, 600 for labels, 400 for reading.
  • Every caption, in caps
  • Eyebrows and labels, tracked 0.18em
  • Explanatory lines on cards
  • The big idea: that belongs to Cormorant
Accent · the hand
Aa
Permanent Marker
Handwritten emphasis for strong opinions and key takeaways. Use sparingly: two or three moments a video.
  • The one takeaway of the video
  • A note scrawled on a taped card
  • A red word over B-roll
  • Captions, body text, or beside a Cormorant headline
Archive · the details
Aa
Special Elite
Archival notes and documentary details, like a typewritten manuscript or an old journal. Used sparingly, one weight only.
  • Kickers, dates and source lines
  • Data labels and small statistics
  • Annotations and catalogue marks
  • Headlines, captions or large numbers

In use

95% minimalism, 5% rebellion: loud type, real surfaces, one red word. Every frame is live HTML at true phone width (360px).

Rules
  • At most two faces per frame, plus Special Elite for a small archival detail.
  • One Cormorant headline per frame. One red word per frame.
  • Permanent Marker two or three times a video, never twice in one frame.
  • Special Elite for dates, sources and labels; big numbers stay in Cormorant.
  • Captions are always Raleway 800 caps, never light weights over footage.
  • Cormorant never below 48px on video: its hairlines disappear.

Your three modes

How each mode looks for you, on a frame of your own footage.

Cards

One frame. One idea. One focal point.

  • Each card is a sequence of four moments, not a static infographic.
  • One dominant headline or number per moment, one supporting visual, one short line.
  • Crop the chart, isolate the number, drop secondary labels.
  • Nothing that carries the story is smaller than 19px on a phone.
  • Ivory washi paper, archival marks, one handwritten takeaway.
  • Smooth, restrained motion: fades, line draws, a slow push.

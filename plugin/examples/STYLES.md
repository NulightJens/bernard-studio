# Your styles

Twelve ways to turn what you say into a post. Open the gallery to watch them: type /studio-styles.

## Reels from your footage

### Screen and face
Your screen on top, you below, and the number or key phrase lands between them from the first frame.

- **Why it works:** A screenshot next to your face is the one format that has grown your Instagram.
- **What to give it:** A talking video of you, plus a screen recording of what you are talking about.
- **To record:** Talk to the camera for 10 to 20 seconds about one thing you did on screen, then record that screen.
- **To make one:** type /studio and say "Screen and face".

### Receipts
Paper receipts land on the claim: what it cost then, what it costs now. A proven reel edit, set in your brand kit.

- **Why it works:** The proof is on screen the moment you say it.
- **What to give it:** A talking video where you compare a before and an after number, plus a screen recording.
- **To record:** Say the old price or time and the new one in one breath, for example "that took eight weeks, now it takes twenty minutes".
- **To make one:** type /studio and say "Receipts".

### Documentary
A wide cinema frame, slow push-ins and candlelight. A figure below counts as you say it.

- **Why it works:** The most cinematic of the set.
- **What to give it:** A talking video. A number in what you say makes it land.
- **To record:** A calm, seated take in a warm room works best. Any of your existing videos can be used.
- **To make one:** type /studio and say "Documentary".

### Words in the room
Your words on the wall behind you, with you in front of them.

- **Why it works:** Premium and calm, and the key words are there from the first frame.
- **What to give it:** A talking video shot on a tripod, with some wall showing behind you.
- **To record:** Keep the camera still and leave space on the wall beside or above your head.
- **To make one:** type /studio and say "Words in the room".

### Cutout
You cut out on black, with a new picture on each key word.

- **Why it works:** The fastest pace in the set.
- **What to give it:** A well-lit talking video, plus screen recordings or photos for the pictures.
- **To record:** Light your face and hair well and wear something that stands apart from the background.
- **To make one:** type /studio and say "Cutout".

## Carousels

### The art of
An object-grid carousel: the topic ringed by real objects and two cutouts of you, then one idea per slide with six captioned objects.

- **Why it works:** A format that is working on Instagram right now, with your face on it twice.
- **What to give it:** A topic and your lines (from one of your videos or typed), and a video or photos of you.
- **To record:** Nothing new is needed. For new poses, a few sharp photos of you against a plain wall.
- **To make one:** type /studio and say "The art of".

### Prompt pack
A raked-sand cover that draws itself while you speak, then your prompts, one per slide, ready to copy.

- **Why it works:** Prompt carousels are your best-performing slideshow type.
- **What to give it:** Your prompts, and one short voice line for the moving cover.
- **To record:** Nothing new is needed if the prompts are in one of your videos.
- **To make one:** type /studio and say "Prompt pack".

### Field notes
Your steps typed on cream paper, with taped prints of your screen and of you.

- **Why it works:** Your process, shown the way you did it.
- **What to give it:** Your steps (from a video or typed), a few screen frames and a photo of you.
- **To record:** Nothing new is needed if you walked through the steps in a video.
- **To make one:** type /studio and say "Field notes".

### Top 5
A countdown list with your real screens, one item per slide.

- **Why it works:** Lists are your biggest format ever.
- **What to give it:** A list of five things, and screen frames or footage for each.
- **To record:** Nothing new is needed if the list comes from one of your videos.
- **To make one:** type /studio and say "Top 5".

## Animated, over your voice

### Zen garden
One overhead shot of black sand. Stones are ringed as you speak, and one red stone rises on your last line.

- **Why it works:** Calm and yours: the garden from your brand origin.
- **What to give it:** A short voice line, 10 to 20 seconds, from any of your videos.
- **To record:** Nothing new is needed.
- **To make one:** type /studio and say "Zen garden".

### Your feed
Your real posts between identical grey ones, until yours lifts out of the feed.

- **Why it works:** Your own work as the proof.
- **What to give it:** A screen recording scrolling through your own posts, and a short voice line.
- **To record:** Record your screen while you scroll slowly through your own posts.
- **To make one:** type /studio and say "Your feed".

### Moving type
Your words set as a moving typeset page, ending on the line that matters, struck or marked in red.

- **Why it works:** Clean and readable with the sound off.
- **What to give it:** A short voice line, 10 to 20 seconds, from any of your videos.
- **To record:** Nothing new is needed.
- **To make one:** type /studio and say "Moving type".
